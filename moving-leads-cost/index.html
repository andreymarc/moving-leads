Use Cost per Booked Move

Cost per booked move = campaign spend / booked moves.

For illustration only: a $600 campaign producing three booked moves has a campaign cost of $200 per booked move. These numbers are an example, not Quick Moving Leads pricing or a performance forecast.

Track sales labor and the value of completed jobs separately to judge whether the campaign works for your business. With zero bookings, there is no cost-per-booking figure to calculate yet.

Moving Lead Cost FAQ

How much do moving leads cost?

Quick Moving Leads provides a quote after reviewing the market, move type, lead model, and delivery requirements. There is no universal rate published on this site. Request current pricing for your company.

Why can exclusive leads cost more than shared leads?

Exclusive distribution supplies the inquiry to one lead buyer, while shared distribution can supply several. Compare the quoted prices alongside competition, contact rates, and cost per booked move.

How are long-distance live transfers priced?

Request a campaign quote with the price per accepted transfer and the agreed call criteria. Confirm routes, screening, receiving hours, missed-call handling, and any minimum commitment before purchasing.

Is there a minimum purchase?

Minimum purchase requirements, caps, and any campaign commitment must be confirmed in your quote. Do not assume a minimum or no-contract arrangement from the information on this page.

Are invalid leads or missed calls credited?

Credit eligibility depends on the applicable campaign agreement. Confirm the definition of an invalid lead or transfer, the evidence needed, and the reporting deadline before launch. Review the site Terms and the written campaign terms.
