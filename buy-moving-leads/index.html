Buying Moving Leads FAQ

Where can moving companies buy leads?

Moving companies can request exclusive leads, shared leads, and live transfers from Quick Moving Leads. Submit a request to review the service areas, move types, pricing, and delivery options available for your company.

Can a small moving company buy leads?

A small moving company can request availability. Start the discussion with your service area, sales capacity, and budget so the campaign can be reviewed against the volume your team can handle.

Should I buy exclusive leads or shared leads?

Exclusive leads reduce competition from other buyers of the same inquiry. Shared leads can offer a lower cost per opportunity but require your team to compete. Compare both using your contact, quote, and booking results.

Do I need a CRM to buy moving leads?

Discuss email delivery or your existing CRM workflow during setup. For live transfers, confirm the receiving phone line and staffed hours. Delivery compatibility is reviewed before the campaign starts.

Are prices and lead volume guaranteed?

Pricing, volume, and availability are confirmed for the campaign. The site does not promise a fixed number of leads or booked jobs. Review the written campaign terms before purchasing.
