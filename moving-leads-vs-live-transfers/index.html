Moving Leads vs. Live Transfers

A form lead gives your moving company an inquiry to follow up. A live transfer connects a moving customer to your sales team by phone. Quick Moving Leads offers form leads for local and long-distance moves, and live transfers for long-distance moves.

Exclusive Is a Separate Decision

Exclusive and shared describe distribution. Form lead and live transfer describe delivery. A live phone conversation does not, by itself, prove that no other moving company has received the customer's inquiry.

When comparing providers, ask how the opportunity is distributed and what the customer has requested. See exclusive vs. shared moving leads for that comparison.

Compare the Same Outcome

Measure accepted opportunities, conversations, quotes, and booked moves for each campaign. Divide campaign spend by booked moves and compare similar markets over the same period.

A live transfer removes an initial contact step, but it does not guarantee a qualified job or a booking. Confirm the campaign's screening and billing rules in your pricing review.

Lead Delivery Questions

Are live transfers better than form moving leads?

It depends on your team. Live transfers suit staffed phone lines that can handle immediate conversations. Form leads suit teams with prompt follow-up and a consistent quoting process. Compare cost per booked move using your own results.

Is every live transfer exclusive?

No. Live transfer describes the call connection, while exclusivity describes lead distribution. Ask the provider to confirm distribution terms for the specific campaign.

Can a moving company use both?

Yes. Request availability for form leads and live transfers, then assign enough staff to work each. Track their delivery, acceptance, and booking results separately.

Which costs less per booked move?

There is no universal answer. Rates, contact rates, qualification, and booking performance vary. Compare campaign spend divided by booked moves over a consistent period and service area.
