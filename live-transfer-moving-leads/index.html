How a Moving Live-Transfer Campaign Starts

A useful campaign begins with agreement on which conversations your team can handle.

  1. Define the Moves You Want

    Review your long-distance routes, move dates, and any size or service restrictions. Confirm availability for those requirements.

  2. Agree on Call Rules

    Confirm what is checked before a transfer, when a call is billable, how duplicates and wrong-area calls are handled, and what happens if your team misses a call.

  3. Prepare Your Sales Team

    Choose staffed delivery hours and a receiving line. Confirm daily caps and routing before calls begin.

  4. Review Accepted Calls and Bookings

    Record which calls match the agreed criteria, how many receive quotes, and how many become booked moves. Use those results to review the campaign.

Calls for Long-Distance and Interstate Routes

Live Transfer Moving Leads FAQ

What are live transfer moving leads?

Live transfer moving leads are phone conversations with people seeking moving services that are connected to a moving company's sales team. The buyer handles the quote and booking process after the connection.

What are your live-transfer operating hours?

Long-distance moving live transfers operate Monday-Friday, 9 AM-5 PM EST. Your sales team should be available to answer during the delivery hours agreed for your campaign within that window.

Are live transfers the same as exclusive moving leads?

No. Live transfer describes how the conversation reaches your team. Exclusive describes how an inquiry is distributed among lead buyers. Confirm exclusivity separately when requesting a live-transfer campaign.

What is checked before a moving call is transferred?

Qualification requirements must be confirmed for your campaign. Ask whether the origin, destination, move date, move size, and requested services are checked before transfer. Do not assume every call includes the same screening.

Are your live transfers for local or long-distance moves?

Quick Moving Leads offers live transfers for long-distance moves. Origin markets, destination routes, and receiving hours are reviewed before confirming availability. For local moves, request exclusive or shared form leads.

How much do moving live transfers cost?

Quick Moving Leads provides pricing after reviewing your market and campaign requirements. Request the rate, minimum commitment, billable-call definition, and applicable credit rules before starting.

What happens if our team misses a transfer?

Missed-call handling and billing depend on the campaign agreement. Confirm the routing, retry process, and any credit eligibility before launch, and choose hours when your team can answer.

Can I buy form leads and live transfers together?

You can request both and review availability for each. Form leads give your team inquiries to follow up; live transfers require representatives available to answer during the agreed delivery hours.
