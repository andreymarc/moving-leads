Information We Collect

Quick Moving Leads may collect information submitted through website forms, phone calls, text messages, email, and CRM setup requests. This can include company name, contact name, email address, phone number, lead type, service area, coverage states, move categories, CRM delivery details, and other information you choose to provide.

The Mini CRM stores inquiry information locally in the browser where it is used. It is not a secure shared database unless a backend is added later.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, check lead availability, discuss moving lead campaigns, configure email or CRM delivery, provide customer support, improve the website, and maintain business records.

Lead Requests

We use submitted information to understand requested markets, lead types, and moving services.

CRM Setup

Setup information is used to configure lead routing, delivery rules, caps, hours, and coverage.

Communication

We may contact you by email, phone, or text in response to your inquiry.

Sharing and Security

Quick Moving Leads does not sell private CRM setup instructions as a standalone product. Information may be shared with service providers used to operate the website, process forms, host the site, communicate with prospects, or deliver requested services.

No website or browser-based tool can guarantee absolute security. Do not submit passwords, private API keys, or sensitive credentials through website forms.

Your Choices

You can contact Quick Moving Leads to request updates or deletion of information you submitted. You can also export and clear Mini CRM browser data directly from the Mini CRM tool.
