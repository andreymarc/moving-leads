When Shared Leads Make Sense

Shared moving leads can work well when you want more chances to quote jobs at a lower cost per lead. They are best for movers with fast response times and a reliable follow-up process.

Unlike exclusive leads, shared leads may go to more than one moving company. The advantage is volume and affordability. The tradeoff is competition.

Best Fit

  • New markets
  • Open calendar capacity
  • Fast phone teams
  • Lower cost per opportunity
  • Movers comfortable competing
