When Shared Leads Fit Your Sales Team

Shared leads can give a moving company more inquiries to work at a lower price per opportunity than exclusive delivery. The tradeoff is that other movers may also contact the customer.

This model fits teams with a clear quoting process, prompt outreach, and consistent follow-up. Review your actual contact and booking rates before increasing the daily volume.

Confirm Before Starting

  • How many companies may receive the same inquiry
  • When inquiries are captured and delivered
  • Which move details are included
  • Service-area and move-type requirements
  • Daily caps, pricing, and duplicate or invalid-lead rules

Shared Moving Leads FAQ

What are shared moving leads?

Shared moving leads are inquiries from people seeking moving services that may be supplied to more than one moving company. Multiple lead buyers may compete for the same move.

How many movers receive a shared lead?

The distribution limit must be confirmed for your campaign. Ask how many buyers may receive an inquiry and whether those buyers cover the same market before purchasing.

Are shared leads cheaper than exclusive leads?

Shared leads generally have a lower price per inquiry because the opportunity can be sold to several buyers. Actual pricing depends on the market and campaign, and the cost per booked move depends on your results.

Can I combine shared and exclusive moving leads?

Yes. Request both and review availability by market. Track each lead model separately so you can compare contact rates, booked moves, and total campaign cost.

How are shared moving leads delivered?

Discuss email delivery or delivery to your existing CRM during the availability review. Confirm the fields included, delivery timing, and any routing requirements before starting.
