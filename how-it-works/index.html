The Moving Lead Process

A clear process helps your sales team know what is coming in, where it came from, and how to follow up.

Step 1

Capture the Request

A customer submits move details through a page built around intent: local, long-distance, packing, storage, route, state, exclusive, shared, or live transfer.

Step 2

Match the Market

The request is matched to the campaign rules: service area, move type, origin, destination, exclusivity, daily cap, and availability.

Step 3

Deliver the Lead

Lead details can be routed to email or CRM delivery based on the setup instructions your team provides.

Step 4

Follow Up Fast

Your team calls or texts quickly with context, which matters for exclusive leads and matters even more for shared leads.

Step 5

Adjust Filters

Markets, routes, daily caps, lead types, and delivery details can be adjusted as campaign needs change.

Step 6

Scale What Works

Use exclusive leads for priority markets and shared leads when volume testing or extra coverage makes sense.

What Buyers Can Control

Good moving lead campaigns are not one-size-fits-all. They should match the jobs your team can quote and service.

  • Exclusive, shared, or a mix of both
  • Local and long-distance move types
  • State, city, metro, and route coverage
  • Packing and storage opportunities
  • Email delivery or CRM delivery
  • Daily cap, business hours, and lead destination
