Moving Leads FAQ

Clear answers for movers comparing exclusive leads, shared leads, live transfers, CRM delivery, and lead cost.

What are moving leads?

Moving leads are customer inquiries from people looking for moving services. They may include local, long-distance, packing, storage, shared, exclusive, or live transfer opportunities.

What is the difference between exclusive and shared moving leads?

Exclusive leads are sent to one moving company. Shared leads may be sent to more than one moving company, so speed-to-contact matters more.

How much do moving leads cost?

Cost depends on lead type, market, delivery method, and exclusivity. Quick Moving Leads quotes availability by market and setup details.

Can leads be sent to my CRM?

Yes. Use the CRM setup page to submit destination, posting instructions, daily cap, coverage, delivery locations, and hours.

Can I request leads by state or route?

Yes. Send the states, metro areas, routes, and service types you want. Quick Moving Leads can review what is available.
