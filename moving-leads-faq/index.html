Buying Moving Leads: Questions and Answers

Answers for moving companies comparing exclusive leads, shared leads, long-distance live transfers, pricing, and delivery.

Ask About Lead Availability
What are moving leads?

Moving leads are inquiries from people seeking moving services. A form lead provides contact and move information for follow-up. A live transfer connects a customer conversation to the moving company's sales team.

Who buys moving leads from Quick Moving Leads?

The service is for moving companies seeking customer opportunities. Quick Moving Leads offers local and long-distance form leads, plus long-distance moving live transfers, subject to campaign availability.

What is the difference between exclusive and shared moving leads?

An exclusive inquiry is distributed to one lead buyer by the provider. A shared inquiry may go to several moving companies. Exclusivity does not mean a customer has never contacted another mover independently.

What are long-distance live transfer moving leads?

They are phone conversations with people seeking long-distance moving services connected to your sales team. Confirm the origin and destination requirements, screening, hours, and billing rules before starting.

Do you offer live transfers for local moving jobs?

Quick Moving Leads offers live transfers for long-distance moves. Local movers can request exclusive or shared form leads for their service areas.

What hours are long-distance live transfers available?

Long-distance live transfers operate Monday-Friday, 9 AM-5 PM EST. Confirm your receiving line and sales-team availability within that window before launch.

How much do moving leads and live transfers cost?

Pricing is quoted after reviewing your market, move type, lead model, and delivery requirements. Confirm the price, any minimum commitment, acceptance rules, and applicable credit terms in the campaign agreement.

Can I choose states or interstate routes?

You can request states, cities, pickup areas, and destination routes. Availability is reviewed for the requested campaign, so a listed location does not guarantee current lead volume.

Can leads be sent to my CRM?

Discuss your CRM or email delivery requirements through the contact form. Compatibility, included fields, caps, and delivery timing are reviewed before launch. For live transfers, confirm the receiving line and staffed hours.

What move details should I confirm before buying?

Ask which details are captured or checked, such as customer contact information, origin, destination, move date, move size, and requested services. Confirm the actual fields and screening used for your campaign.

Do purchased leads guarantee booked moves?

No. A lead or connected conversation is an opportunity. Bookings depend on service fit, customer needs, pricing, availability, and your sales team's follow-up.

How can I request moving leads?

Use the contact form to send your company name, contact details, and preferred lead type. Quick Moving Leads will review your market, delivery needs, and current pricing with you.
