Exclusive vs Shared Moving Leads

Both lead types can work. The right choice depends on your budget, market, sales speed, and how much competition you want on each inquiry.

Ask Which Fits My Market

Exclusive Moving Leads

Exclusive leads are sent to one moving company. They usually cost more, but they reduce direct competition and can produce a cleaner quoting process.

  • Best for priority markets
  • Better for higher-value moves
  • Less competition on each inquiry
  • Strong fit for experienced sales teams
View exclusive leads

Shared Moving Leads

Shared leads may be sent to more than one moving company. They usually cost less and can create more total quote opportunities.

  • Best for more volume
  • Useful for open calendar capacity
  • Requires fast contact
  • Good for testing new markets
View shared leads

A Smart Campaign Can Use Both

Many movers use exclusive leads for core markets and shared leads for extra volume. This lets you protect your best service areas while still finding opportunities where you have capacity.

Core Market

Use exclusive leads where you have trucks, crews, and strong closing power.

Growth Market

Use shared leads to test demand before paying more for exclusivity.

Seasonal Push

Use both when moving demand rises and your team can handle more inquiries.
