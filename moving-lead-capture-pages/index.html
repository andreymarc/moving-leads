Capture Pages Built Around Intent

A strong moving lead page should not just collect a name and phone number. It should match the visitor's move type, location, timing, and service need so the lead buyer can follow up with context.

These examples show the kind of funnels Quick Moving Leads can support while keeping the offer clear and easy to verify.

Common Capture Fields

  • Pickup city and state
  • Destination city and state
  • Move date or timing
  • Move size or property type
  • Packing or storage need
  • Phone, email, and consent language

Example Capture Funnels

Each funnel can be matched to exclusive, shared, or CRM-delivered lead flow depending on market availability.

Local Move Quote Page

For customers moving inside one city, metro, or service radius.

  • Pickup ZIP
  • Move size
  • Move date

Long-Distance Route Page

For interstate and cross-country customers with higher-value routes.

  • Origin state

Packing & Storage Page

  • Packing need
  • Storage timing

State Market Page

  • State coverage

  • Lead model

Live Transfer Page

For teams that can answer quickly and work a live customer conversation.

  • Call intent

Exclusive Lead Page

For priority markets where the buyer wants less competition per inquiry.

  • Market filter
  • Move category
  • CRM delivery

How Leads Can Flow

The page, form, and delivery setup should work together so the sales team knows what to do next.

1. Visitor Converts

The capture page matches the visitor's move intent and collects the right contact details.

2. Lead Is Routed

Submissions can go to email, CRM posting, or setup rules based on the campaign.

3. Team Follows Up

The mover receives enough context to call, text, quote, and qualify the opportunity.
