Before You Submit

Use this setup form when your account is ready for delivery or when you need to change routing. Keep the information practical and specific so leads can be sent to the right place the first time.

  • Use a CRM inbox, posting endpoint, or lead-grabbing email you actively monitor
  • Set a realistic daily cap based on your sales team's call speed
  • List only the pickup and delivery markets you can service
  • Include operating hours so live transfers and follow-up expectations are clear
